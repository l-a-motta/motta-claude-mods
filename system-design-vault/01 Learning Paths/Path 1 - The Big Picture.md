> [!path] 🌱 The Big Picture
> **Who it's for:** Anyone, no tech background needed.
> **Time:** ≈ 2–3 hours · **Lessons:** 29
> **Goal:** Understand how big apps and AI products are built, well enough to follow a conversation with engineers.

## How to take this path

- Go top to bottom. Each lesson is one note; the 🌱 basics notes take about 5 minutes each.
- **Tick the box** when you finish a lesson. Obsidian saves it, so you can stop and come back.
- Each note ends with **Check yourself** questions. Try them before peeking at the answers.
- Stuck on a word? Hover it, or look it up in the [[Glossary]].

## Lessons

- [ ] **1.** [[Requirements]] — Pinning down what the system must do and how well, before building. *(Curious? [[Requirements (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **2.** [[Trade-offs]] — Every design choice gives something up; good design picks knowingly. *(Curious? [[Trade-offs (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **3.** [[Networking]] — How computers find each other and exchange messages. *(Curious? [[Networking (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **4.** [[Databases]] — Systems that store data safely and let you query it later. *(Curious? [[Databases (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*

### 🏛️ Architecture
- [ ] **5.** [[Monoliths]] — One program that does everything, deployed as a single unit. *(Curious? [[Monoliths (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **6.** [[Microservices]] — Many small services, each owning one job, talking over the network. *(Curious? [[Microservices (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **7.** [[SQL Databases]] — Tables with strict structure, joined and queried with SQL. *(Curious? [[SQL Databases (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **8.** [[NoSQL Databases]] — Flexible databases built for scale and specific access patterns. *(Curious? [[NoSQL Databases (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*

### 📈 Scalability
- [ ] **9.** [[Caching]] — Keeping a copy of frequently used data somewhere faster. *(Curious? [[Caching (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **10.** [[CDN]] — Servers around the world that hand out content close to each user. *(Curious? [[CDN (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **11.** [[Load Balancing]] — Spreading incoming work across many servers so none is overwhelmed. *(Curious? [[Load Balancing (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **12.** [[Message Queues]] — Buffers that let parts of a system hand off work without waiting. *(Curious? [[Message Queues (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
- [ ] **13.** [[Fault Tolerance]] — Designing so that when something breaks, the system keeps going. *(Curious? [[Fault Tolerance (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]])*
