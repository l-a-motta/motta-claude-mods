> [!path] ⚡ LLM App Builder
> **Who it's for:** Builders shipping chatbots, copilots or agents on top of LLM APIs.
> **Time:** ≈ 6–8 hours · **Lessons:** 14
> **Goal:** The shortest route to designing a solid LLM application.

## How to take this path

- Go top to bottom. Each lesson is one note; read the 🌱 basics note, then its 🔬 deep dive.
- **Tick the box** when you finish a lesson. Obsidian saves it, so you can stop and come back.
- Each note ends with **Check yourself** questions. Try them before peeking at the answers.
- Stuck on a word? Hover it, or look it up in the [[Glossary]].

## Lessons

### 📈 Scalability
- [ ] **1.** [[API Design]] — Designing the contract other programs use to talk to yours. Then [[API Design (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].
- [ ] **2.** [[Caching]] — Keeping a copy of frequently used data somewhere faster. Then [[Caching (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].
- [ ] **3.** [[Rate Limiting]] — Capping how often someone can call you, to stay fair and safe. Then [[Rate Limiting (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].

### 🧩 AI Building Blocks
- [ ] **4.** [[AI Model Types]] — The main families of models and what each is good for. Then [[AI Model Types (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].

### 🤖 Modern LLM Systems
- [ ] **5.** [[Context Engineering]] — Deciding exactly what goes into a model's input so it answers well. Then [[Context Engineering (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].
- [ ] **6.** [[Embeddings & Vector Databases]] — Turning meaning into numbers so you can search by similarity. Then [[Embeddings & Vector Databases (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].
- [ ] **7.** [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] — Letting a model look things up before it answers. Then [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].
- [ ] **8.** [[AI Agents & Tool Use]] — Models that plan, call tools and take steps toward a goal. Then [[AI Agents & Tool Use (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].
- [ ] **9.** [[LLM Inference & Optimization]] — Making large models answer fast and cheaply. Then [[LLM Inference & Optimization (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].

### ☂️ The 12 Ribs
- [ ] **10.** [[Evaluation & Metrics]] — Measuring whether a model is actually good, before and after launch. Then [[Evaluation & Metrics (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].

### 🤖 Modern LLM Systems
- [ ] **11.** [[Guardrails & AI Safety]] — Checks around a model that keep its inputs and outputs safe. Then [[Guardrails & AI Safety (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].

### ☂️ The 12 Ribs

- [ ] **13.** [[Cost Optimization]] — Getting the same results for less money. Then [[Cost Optimization (Deep Dive)|🔬 deep dive]].
